# Brightway Facilities: Office Cleaning Services Agreement

Client: Fernhill Studio Ltd. Contractor: Brightway Facilities Ltd. Signed 9 December 2024.
(Invented for the recipe sample.)

2.1 Term. This agreement starts on 1 January 2025 and continues for a minimum term of 24 months.

2.2 After the minimum term, this agreement continues from month to month until either party ends it
by giving not less than one calendar month's written notice, expiring on the last day of a calendar
month. Notice may end the agreement on the last day of the minimum term.

2.3 Notices under this agreement must be given by letter delivered to the other party's registered
office. Notice by email is not valid notice.

5.1 Fees. GBP 1,150.00 per month, excluding VAT, invoiced monthly in arrears.

5.2 Payment. Each invoice is payable within 30 days from the end of the month in which it is dated.
