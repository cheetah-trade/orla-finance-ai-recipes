# How we want payments approved (Maya Ellison, CEO, 20 November 2026)

(Invented for the recipe sample: the company and the people are not real.)

Our approval rules in Orla are in US dollars, so these are too.

1. Under $1,000: no signature needed.
2. From $1,000 up to $10,000: one signature, by the owner or an admin.
3. $10,000 and over: two signatures.
4. The first payment to a new supplier always needs one signature, whatever the amount.
5. Whoever adds or changes a supplier's bank details may not approve payments to that supplier.
6. Ana, our external bookkeeper, prepares the books and never pays or starts a payment.
7. Nobody approves their own payment.
8. Everyone who can approve a payment signs in with two-factor.
9. People who have left lose access on their last day.
