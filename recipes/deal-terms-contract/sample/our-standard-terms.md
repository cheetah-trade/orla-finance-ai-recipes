# Fernhill Studio Ltd: our standard commercial terms (internal)

(Invented for the recipe sample.)

1. Prices are those of the current price list, in GBP, excluding VAT. VAT is added at the rate in
   force on the invoice date.
2. Payment: 30 days from the invoice date. Up to 45 days may be agreed by the account lead. Anything
   longer needs the CEO's written approval.
3. Discounts: up to 10% on any one line may be agreed by the account lead. More than 10% needs the
   CEO's written approval.
4. Invoicing: workshops and build days monthly in arrears for the days worked; support monthly in
   advance.
5. Term: as agreed per deal. Automatic renewal only if the client asks for it.
6. Liability cap: the total fees paid under the contract in the 12 months before the claim. No
   unlimited liability. Any change needs the CEO and a lawyer.
7. Governing law of our template: England and Wales. Any change needs a lawyer.
8. Clauses outside our template are not added by the account lead: they go to the CEO and, where
   they touch liability, price protection or exclusivity, to a lawyer.
