Opening a business account: what we need from a UK limited company
(copied from the bank's onboarding page on 20 November 2026)

1. Certificate of incorporation.
2. Company register extract dated within the last 3 months, showing directors and persons with
   significant control.
3. For every director, and every person who owns or controls 25% or more of the company directly or
   indirectly: a valid passport or national identity card. Driving licences are not accepted as
   identity documents. The document must be valid for at least 3 months after you apply.
4. For the same people: proof of home address dated within the last 3 months (utility bill, bank
   statement, council tax bill).
5. Proof of the company's trading address dated within the last 3 months.
6. An ownership chart down to the individuals, with percentages at every level.
7. A description of the business: what you sell, to whom, in which countries, and your website.
8. Expected monthly money in and money out for the first 12 months, and how you estimated it.
9. Where the first deposit will come from, with a document.

The names and addresses on all documents must match the company register. If they differ, tell us
why. We may ask for more documents. Opening an account is at the bank's discretion.
